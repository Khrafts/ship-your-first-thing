# Module 7 — Where to go from here

This is the last module, and the shortest. Nothing here runs and nothing touches your app. It names the parts of the course that were never about the app you learned in, then gives you a short list of what might be worth learning next, with permission to skip most of it.

## What you'll be able to do

- Tell which of three moves you need when you are stuck: a fresh conversation, a different agent, or leaving the keyboard.
- Say which parts of the way you work carry over to any agent app, and ask the six questions that locate them in one you have never opened.
- Pick from five next topics, each with who should follow it and who should skip it, including picking none.

## Lessons

1. [`01-session-hygiene.md`](./01-session-hygiene.md) — starting fresh, moving to a different agent, and leaving the keyboard: which one, and when
2. [`02-the-loop-on-other-agents.md`](./02-the-loop-on-other-agents.md) — what stays the same in any agent app, what changes, and the questions to ask a new one
3. [`03-where-to-go-from-here.md`](./03-where-to-go-from-here.md) — what not to learn next, and five pointers with who should follow each

Read the first two now, while the work is fresh. Come back to the third on the day you want to know whether a thing is worth your afternoon.
